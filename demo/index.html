Placement Promise Card

Abishikth Ebenezer · research prototype · November 2026

Paid placement, internship and "job guarantee" offers describe their promise in many different ways. A Promise Card answers eight questions a candidate should be able to answer before paying, and marks each answer stated, vague, contradictory, not stated or not applicable, with exact quotes. A language model drafts the card; code removes any quote that is not in the source and tightens answers that rest on open-ended wording. The card never judges whether an offer is fair, legal or genuine.
