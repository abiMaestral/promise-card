What I learned

  • The AI's most common mistake is calling vague terms clear (21 of 200). For a tool meant to warn you, that's the worst direction to be wrong.
  • It found none of the 5 contradictions inside a single document, like a fee table and an FAQ on the same page giving different drop-out fees.
  • When I re-labelled 10 offers a day later, I changed 15 of 80 answers myself, mostly between "stated" and "vague". That line is genuinely hard, so the card always shows the quote next to its stamp.
  • My own rule had a bug: it stamped "we may modify these terms" as vague, when for "Can terms change?" that line is the clear answer. The evaluation caught it, and both scores are shown.
  • Giving the AI my full labelling rules made it worse (68.5% → 64.5%): it called even more vague terms clear. More instructions didn't fix the judgement.
  • Next: a check for contradictions inside one page, and more than one person labelling.
