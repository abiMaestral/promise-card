Placement Promise Card

Abishikth Ebenezer · built for the ATRISI Amplify Builder Challenge · November 2026

Paid "job guarantee", placement and internship offers ask freshers to pay first. Before paying, you should be able to answer eight questions: how much, when, what exactly is promised, what you must do, whether terms can change, what happens if you're not placed, whether you can refuse an offer, and whether some terms are hidden in another document. This tool answers them from the offer's own words, and says vague or not stated when the offer doesn't really say.

"Check the wording" runs in your browser, with no AI and no data sent anywhere. It finds amounts, open-ended phrases and pointers to documents you haven't been shown.

What I learned from the numbers

  • The AI's most common mistake is calling vague terms clear (21 of 200). For a tool that is supposed to warn you, that's the worst direction to be wrong.
  • It found none of the 5 contradictions inside a single document (for example a fee table and an FAQ on the same page giving different drop-out fees).
  • When I re-labelled 10 offers a day later, I changed 15 of 80 answers myself, mostly between "stated" and "vague". That line is genuinely hard, so the card always shows the quote next to its verdict.
  • My own rule had a bug: it marked "we may modify these terms" as vague, when for "Can terms change?" that sentence is the clear answer. The evaluation caught it; both scores are shown.
Code, method and data notes: see the GitHub README. Offers were saved 22–24 Nov 2026; several pages have changed since. This tool reports how clearly an offer is written. It does not say whether an offer is fair, legal or genuine.
